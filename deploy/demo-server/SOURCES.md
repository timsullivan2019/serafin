# Demo library sources

Every title on the demo server is a Blender open film released under a Creative Commons Attribution licence, so the films, their TMDB posters (the films' own artwork) and screenshots of the app showing them can all be used with credit. `fetch-media.sh` downloads exactly these sources.

All films are © Blender Foundation. Credit line: (CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org.

## Shows

Caminandes, directed by Pablo Vazquez, as one season of three episodes.

| Episode | Licence | Licence confirmed at | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S01E01 Llama Drama (2013) | CC BY 3.0 | licence field on the [archive.org item](https://archive.org/details/Caminandes1LlamaDrama) | [archive.org](https://archive.org/download/Caminandes1LlamaDrama/01_llama_drama_1080p.mp4). It is no longer on YouTube. |
| S01E02 Gran Dillama (2013) | CC BY | [studio.blender.org, Caminandes 2](https://studio.blender.org/projects/caminandes-2/) | [Debian's mirror of download.blender.org](https://cdimage.debian.org/mirror/blender.org/demo/movies/caminandes_gran_dillama.mp4.zip) |
| S01E03 Llamigos (2016) | CC BY | YouTube licence field on the source video | [YouTube, Blender Studio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L6mLFxGRFI4) |

TMDB lists the three films as separate movies, not as a series, so the show's title, overview and episode overviews come from NFO files written by `fetch-media.sh`, and its poster and backdrop are stills from Llamigos (at 1:56 and 0:14), cropped and scaled by ffmpeg.
