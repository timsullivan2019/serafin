Serafin

A native app for watching your Jellyfin library on iPhone and iPad.

Your movies and shows, with their artwork front and center. Serafin plays them with Picture in Picture and AirPlay, finds them from Spotlight and Siri, and talks only to your own server. It collects nothing about you.
